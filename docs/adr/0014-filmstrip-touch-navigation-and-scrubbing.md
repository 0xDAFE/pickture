# 0014. Filmstrip Touch Navigation, Velocity-Throttled Scrubbing, and Interactive Grid Transitions

Pickture's Filmstrip view supports Apple Photos-style touch navigation alongside its photography culling workflows (`SwipeMode` and `BorderTapNavigation`) through three coordinated mechanisms:

1. **Multiplexed Main-Canvas Gestures with Edge-Pan Handoff**: At `1.0x` scale, a 1-finger horizontal drag pages across a live 3-item carousel (`previous`, `current`, `next`) when `SwipeMode` is off, and executes rating card swipes when `SwipeMode` is on. When zoomed in (`scale > 1.0`), 1-finger drag pans within the image's true fitted aspect bounds and seamlessly hands off into horizontal paging once the pan reaches the left or right image edge. Stationary taps in the outer border zones (`BorderTapNavigation`) execute instant 0ms cuts for rapid A/B burst comparison without intercepting drags or pinches that originate near the screen edge.
2. **Velocity-Throttled `FilmstripScrubber`**: The `FilmstripScrubber` anchors the active `MediaItem` at a centered playhead and supports continuous 1:1 drag scrubbing and momentum scrolling (horizontally in `.bottom` dock, vertically in `.right` dock). During high-velocity scrubbing, the main viewport immediately displays in-memory previews or cached thumbnails while suppressing new full-resolution `ImageDecoderActor` decodes and speculative prefetching until scrub velocity drops below threshold or the gesture settles, preventing I/O saturation on external and network storage volumes (`RemovableMedia` and `NetworkShare`).
3. **Interactive Hero Transition to Grid**: `GridContentView` keeps the active `MediaItem` scrolled into its visible viewport while navigating in Filmstrip view so that pinching below `1.0x` scale—or performing a 1-finger vertical pull-down at `1.0x` scale (enabled when `SwipeMode` is off, and configurable via a code flag for `SwipeMode`)—interactively tracks the user's fingers and shrinks the active item directly into its target Grid cell frame.
